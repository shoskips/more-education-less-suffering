In a society in which resources and goods are distributed fairly, some people would have more than others, because some people need more than others. Communism isn't necessarily about total equality, it's more about the process of equalising, a process that would be continually ongoing. 
Total equality would mean everyone was the same, and everyone would have the same, which is actually a very unfair system, and, is something that is already seen with regards to how people are equally subject to the rules of money and finance, and the free market, actually creates a more unequal system. 
With regards to people such as the disabled, the elderly, etc, they might need more help and support, and therefore they might have more than others because of that.
Liberal equality means everyone is treated the same regardless of their needs and abilities, anarchism would treat people differently based on their needs and abilities, which is, in my opinion, true equality.

Well, by treating people differently I mean giving those that are disadvantaged more support, but not giving preference to the more abled, just to clarify.
Basically levelling the playing field
